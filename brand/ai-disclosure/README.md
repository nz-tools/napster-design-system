# AI disclosure marks

Napster's own marks for disclosing AI-generated actors. They are finished artwork. Use them as supplied whenever a piece needs the disclosure; do not commission or draw new ones.

## What's in this folder

Two forms, each in two colors.

| File | Form | Color | Size (px) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `napster-ai-element-mark-black.png` | The AI mark | Black `#1A1A1A` | 1152×930 |
| `napster-ai-element-mark-white.png` | The AI mark | White `#FAF9F6` | 1152×930 |
| `napster-ai-element-sentence-mono-black.png` | The sentence | Black `#1A1A1A` | 6895×441 |
| `napster-ai-element-sentence-mono-white.png` | The sentence | White `#FAF9F6` | 6895×441 |

All four are PNGs with a transparent background and a small margin built into the canvas.

- **The AI mark.** The letters *AI* inside a rounded outline. The compact form.
- **The sentence.** *Includes AI-generated actors.* on one line, set in IBM Plex Mono, the system's metadata face. The explicit form.

## Treatment

- **Pick the color by background.** White on dark surfaces, black on light ones. Same rule as the logo.
- **Use the files as supplied.** Do not redraw the mark, retype the sentence, reword it, recolor either one, or add effects.
- **Scale proportionally.** Never stretch or crop into the built-in margin.
